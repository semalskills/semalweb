Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our products and services

How fresh is your chicken?

+
Our chicken is processed fresh daily and delivered to you within hours of preparation. We never sell chicken that has been stored for more than 24 hours.

What is your delivery time?

+
We offer same-day delivery for orders placed before 2 PM. Delivery times are typically between 6-8 PM for evening orders and 10-12 PM for morning orders.

Do you offer bulk orders?

+
Yes, we offer special pricing for bulk orders over 10kg. Please contact us directly at 071 183 8661 to discuss your requirements and get a custom quote.

What areas do you deliver to?

+
We deliver to Deraniyagala town and surrounding areas including Kegalle Road, Avissawella Road, and Ruwanwella Road. Contact us for outskirts delivery options.

How do I pay for my order?

+
We accept cash on delivery, bank transfers, and mobile payments. Payment is due at the time of delivery unless arranged otherwise for regular customers.

Can I customize my order?

+
Absolutely! We can accommodate special cutting preferences, portion sizes, and specific packaging requirements. Just mention your needs in the order form.
