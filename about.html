Our Farm Story

From humble beginnings to becoming Deraniyagala's most trusted source for fresh chicken products. Discover our journey of quality, commitment, and community service.

A Legacy of Quality

Ajith Farm Shop was established in 2015 with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide Deraniyagala with the freshest, highest quality chicken products while maintaining the highest standards of hygiene and customer service.

What started as a small family business has grown into a trusted local institution, serving hundreds of satisfied customers across Deraniyagala and surrounding areas. Our commitment to quality, freshness, and customer satisfaction remains unchanged.

Today, we continue to honor our founding principles by sourcing only the best local poultry, maintaining strict quality control standards, and ensuring every customer receives products that meet our exacting standards of excellence.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped our commitment to excellence

2015

Humble Beginnings

Ajith Farm Shop opened its doors with a commitment to provide fresh, quality chicken products to the local community. Starting with basic cuts and a focus on customer service.

2017

Quality Certification

Achieved local health and safety certifications, establishing our reputation for hygienic processing and quality assurance. Expanded product range to meet growing demand.

2019

Home Delivery Launch

Introduced home delivery service to make fresh chicken more accessible to families across Deraniyagala. This service quickly became popular among busy households.

2022

Digital Expansion

Launched online ordering system and expanded delivery network. Modernized processing facilities while maintaining traditional quality standards.

2024

Community Leader

Today, we're proud to be Deraniyagala's most trusted chicken shop, serving hundreds of satisfied customers with the same commitment to quality that started our journey.

Our Quality Process

From farm to your table, every step is carefully managed to ensure the highest quality and freshness in every product we deliver.

1

Sourcing

We partner with trusted local farms that meet our strict quality standards. All chickens are raised in healthy, natural conditions.

2

Processing

Daily processing in our hygienic facility using modern equipment. Every cut is prepared with precision and care.

3

Quality Check

Rigorous quality inspection at every stage. We check for freshness, proper packaging, and adherence to food safety standards.

4

Delivery

Fresh products delivered to your doorstep in temperature-controlled containers to maintain optimal freshness.
